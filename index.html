Phish-N-Dixon

Oopsy, you got caught.

This initiative is an internal, simulated cybersecurity campaign run by IT. Periodic, harmless phishing emails mimicking real-world social engineering tactics—such as fake HR announcements, urgent hardware updates, or spoofed vendor invoices—will be sent to employees.

When an employee clicks a link or submits credentials on a simulated page, no data is compromised. Instead, it serves as a immediate, low-stakes learning moment to reinforce email security habits and keep Dixon’s manufacturing systems safe.
